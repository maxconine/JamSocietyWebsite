JAM SOCIETY

STYLE GUIDE

Blue and white. Square edges. Big Black Ops One in odd places. Home facts sit on a staff like a setlist — not in floating boxes.

PRINCIPLES

  1. 1

    Blue and white only

    White pages. Navy chrome. Action blue for buttons and links. Gray is for body type, not for bars.

  2. 2

    Go huge with the weird font

    Black Ops One is not a 24px nav-only lockup. Slam it on section titles, home labels, and empty corners. Roboto handles the reading.

  3. 3

    Square and sharp

    No pills, no rounded cards. The room is amps and cases. Hover is a color shift.

  4. 4

    It should feel like music

    Staff lines, notes, clefs, guitars, drums. Use them as structure, not sticker-bombed clipart.
